About The Law

What Is The NDPA 2023?

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 was signed into law by President Bola Ahmed Tinubu in June 2023. It is the primary law regulating how personal information is collected, stored, processed, and shared in Nigeria. It replaced the old Nigeria Data Protection Regulation of 2019 and is overseen by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Any individual, business, or organisation that collects or processes the personal data of Nigerian citizens is legally required to comply with the NDPA 2023. This includes software applications, websites, mobile apps, hospitals, banks, schools, and government agencies.

Data must only be collected with explicit user consent
All stored personal data must be encrypted
Data breaches must be reported within 72 hours
Users must be able to delete their personal data
Personal data must not be transferred abroad without safeguards
Fines of up to N10 million for non-compliance
