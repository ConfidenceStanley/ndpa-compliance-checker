SOFTWARE: HealthBridge Patient Management System
VERSION: 1.0
DEVELOPER: HealthBridge Technologies Ltd

DATA COLLECTION:
The system collects patient full name, email address,
phone number, date of birth, and medical history.
The system also collects health insurance details
and next of kin information.
Users must provide consent via a checkbox before
their data is collected and processed.
The consent form clearly states the purpose of
data collection.

SECURITY:
All passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage.
The system uses HTTPS for all data transmission.
However the system does not use encryption for
stored patient records in the database.
There are no access controls on the admin panel.
Any staff member can access all patient records
without restriction.

DATA BREACH:
The system has no formal data breach notification plan.
Users and regulators are not notified in the event
of a data breach.

USER RIGHTS:
Patients can request access to their medical records
through the patient portal.
Patients can correct their personal information
through the profile settings page.
There is no mechanism for patients to delete
their account or personal data.
Patients cannot withdraw their consent once given.

DATA STORAGE:
Patient data is stored on servers in Frankfurt Germany.
No data transfer agreements are in place for this
international transfer.
Data is retained for 10 years regardless of whether
the patient is still active.

DATA SHARING:
The system does not sell patient data to third parties.
Patient data is shared with insurance companies
without explicit patient consent.
A privacy policy is published on the website
explaining data collection practices.

THIRD PARTY:
No written data processing agreements exist with
third party service providers.
The system uses Google Analytics which places
tracking cookies without user consent.

COMPLIANCE:
No Data Protection Officer has been appointed.
No Data Protection Impact Assessment has been
conducted despite processing sensitive health data.
